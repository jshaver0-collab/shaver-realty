If you’re looking at homes for sale in Grapevine, TX and prefer a quieter setting with larger homes, Meritage is worth a closer look. Most of the houses in this small community were built in the early to mid-2000s and sit on cul-de-sac or interior lots. The neighborhood stays relatively low-profile compared to some of the bigger surrounding subdivisions, which is part of its appeal for many buyers.

Location is practical. Meritage sits near Glade Parks, so residents have relatively easy access to shopping, dining, and the Highway 121 corridor without being right on top of the commercial activity. Students attend Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School in the Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. For sellers in Meritage, the combination of home size, GCISD zoning, and proximity to Glade Parks tends to be the strongest marketing angle when listing a home in this part of Grapevine.
