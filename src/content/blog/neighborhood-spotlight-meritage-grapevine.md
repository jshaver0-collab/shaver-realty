Meritage is a smaller, quieter neighborhood in Grapevine that tends to appeal to buyers looking for larger homes in Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Most of the homes were built in the early to mid-2000s and sit on cul-de-sac and interior lots. The community is located near Glade Parks, which gives residents relatively easy access to shopping, dining, and the Highway 121 corridor.

Students attend Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School. Homes in Meritage are generally on the larger side for the immediate area, and the neighborhood has maintained a low-profile residential feel. For sellers, the combination of home size, GCISD zoning, and proximity to Glade Parks can be a practical selling point when marketing homes for sale in Grapevine, TX.
