When people start looking at homes for sale in Grapevine, TX and want something newer, Vineyard Creek Estates often comes up. Most of the homes in this community were built between 2014 and 2017 by Emerald Homes (a D.R. Horton brand) and Plantation Homes. The floor plans tend to be more modern than many of the surrounding neighborhoods that were built a decade or more earlier.

The community is located near Hall Johnson Road and Highway 121, which makes it relatively convenient for reaching Historic Downtown Grapevine, Grapevine Mills, and the broader DFW highway system. Students attend Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School in the Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Sellers in Vineyard Creek Estates usually find that the newer construction and central Grapevine location are the two points buyers respond to most.
