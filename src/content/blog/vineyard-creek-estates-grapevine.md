Vineyard Creek Estates is a newer residential community in Grapevine that appeals to buyers looking for modern homes in Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Built primarily between 2014 and 2017 by builders including Emerald Homes (D.R. Horton) and Plantation Homes, the neighborhood offers a range of floor plans on lots that are generally larger than many of the surrounding subdivisions from earlier decades.

Homes in Vineyard Creek Estates typically feature open layouts, updated finishes, and outdoor living spaces. The community is located near Hall Johnson Road and Highway 121, giving residents relatively quick access to Historic Downtown Grapevine, Grapevine Mills, and the broader DFW highway network. Students attend Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School.
