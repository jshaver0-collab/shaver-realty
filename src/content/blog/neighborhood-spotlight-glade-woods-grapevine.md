Glade Woods is a residential neighborhood in Grapevine located near Glade Road. Homes here are zoned to Grapevine-Colleyville ISD, with students attending Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School. The location offers convenient access to the Glade Road corridor, nearby shopping, and routes toward Historic Downtown Grapevine.
