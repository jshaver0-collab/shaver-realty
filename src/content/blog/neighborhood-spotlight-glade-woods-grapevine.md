Glade Woods is a residential neighborhood in Grapevine near Glade Road. Homes here are zoned to Grapevine-Colleyville ISD, with students attending Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School. The location gives residents practical access to the Glade Road corridor for shopping and dining, as well as routes toward Historic Downtown Grapevine.

Public details on the exact build years and size of Glade Woods are more limited than some of the larger named subdivisions. Buyers searching for homes for sale in Grapevine, TX often evaluate it when they want GCISD schools and a convenient location near established commercial areas without being in one of the bigger master-planned communities. For sellers, the school district and central Grapevine location are usually the strongest points to highlight.
