Starlight Court is a smaller residential community in the Grapevine area that doesn’t get as much attention as the larger named subdivisions. Homes here are zoned to Grapevine-Colleyville ISD, with students attending Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School. The compact size contributes to a quieter residential feel, which is often what draws people to it when they’re searching for homes for sale in Grapevine, TX.

Public information on Starlight Court is limited, which is common for smaller street-level neighborhoods. Buyers who are focused on GCISD and prefer a lower-profile setting sometimes end up looking here after reviewing the more well-known Grapevine communities. For sellers, the main points to emphasize are usually the school district and the quieter Grapevine location.
