If you’ve been looking at **homes for sale in Grapevine, TX**, Shadow Glen is one of the established pockets that offers a quieter residential feel with convenient access to the rest of the city. Mature trees, settled streets, and a straightforward location make it a practical choice for many buyers.

## An Established Grapevine Community

Shadow Glen is a residential neighborhood of single-family homes built primarily in the 1980s and 1990s. Most houses are three- or four-bedroom plans, typically ranging from about 1,800 to 2,800 square feet, with some larger custom or expanded homes mixed in. Lots are generally standard suburban size, giving the streets a comfortable, lived-in character rather than a dense new-construction feel.

Buyers who look here usually care about updates (kitchen, baths, HVAC, roof) and the overall condition of the property more than a brand-new floor plan. Sellers who have already handled the major systems and curb appeal tend to stand out.

## Location, Location, Location

Shadow Glen sits in Grapevine with easy access to everyday shopping, Historic Downtown Grapevine, and the highway network toward Dallas, Fort Worth, and DFW Airport. It’s the kind of location that works well if you want quieter internal streets without being far from the amenities and employment centers that draw people to the area.

## Schools

Addresses in Shadow Glen are in Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Specific campus assignments depend on the exact address. Always confirm the current elementary, middle, and high school feed with GCISD before you buy or list — boundaries can change.
