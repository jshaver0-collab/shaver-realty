If you’ve been looking at **homes for sale in Grapevine, TX**, Eastwoods is one of the smaller, newer pockets that doesn’t show up on every map. Built starting around 2016, it’s a compact enclave of custom-style single-family homes with a quiet street pattern and proximity to Glade Crossing Park.

## A Newer, Compact Grapevine Pocket

Eastwoods was developed as a limited set of homes (roughly 20–26 at build-out) by Cooper Street Homes. Most houses are traditional two-story designs with brick and stone exteriors, typically in the 3,000–4,000 square foot range. Lots generally run about 0.15–0.20 acre, giving the streets a more open feel than denser recent builds.

Buyers looking at **Grapevine real estate** here usually focus on condition, updates, and backyard usability. Because the neighborhood is small and relatively new, well-maintained homes tend to stand out when they come available.

## Location

Eastwoods sits in south-central Grapevine with easy access to Glade Road shopping, Historic Downtown Grapevine, and DFW Airport. The quieter internal streets are the main draw if you want newer construction without a large master-planned feel.

## Schools

Addresses in Eastwoods are in Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Recent listings have shown Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School. Always confirm the current zoning for a specific address with GCISD — boundaries can shift.
