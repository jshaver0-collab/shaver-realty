If you’ve been looking at homes for sale in Grapevine, TX, Belfort Estates is a compact, late-1990s pocket that sits just off Heritage Avenue. It’s the kind of neighborhood that feels established without being overly large — quieter streets, bigger floor plans, and a location that keeps everyday Grapevine amenities close.

## An Established, Compact Community

Homes in Belfort Estates were built primarily in the late 1990s. Most are two-story single-family houses in the 3,000–4,000 square foot range, typically with four or five bedrooms. Lots generally run a quarter-acre or a bit more, which gives the streets a more open feel than denser newer subdivisions.

Buyers who look here usually care about the size of the house, the condition of updates, and how the backyard works for Texas weather. Sellers who have already addressed roof, HVAC, kitchen, and landscaping tend to get more attention.

## Location, Location, Location

Belfort Estates is reached from Hall Johnson Road via Heritage Avenue. That puts it in a practical part of Grapevine — close to everyday shopping and dining, a short drive to Historic Downtown Grapevine, and with straightforward access to the highway network and DFW Airport. It’s convenient without sitting on a busy through street.

## Schools

Addresses in Belfort Estates are in Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Recent listings have shown the feed as Heritage Elementary, Cross Timbers Middle School, and Grapevine High School. School zones can change, so confirm the current assignment for any specific address with GCISD before you buy or list.
