If you’ve been searching **homes for sale in Grapevine, TX**, Stone Bridge Oaks is the low-maintenance option that sits tucked off Glade Road between Highway 360 and Highway 121. It mixes townhomes and detached patio/villa homes, with an HOA that handles exterior and landscape maintenance.

## Low-Maintenance Living in Grapevine

Stone Bridge Oaks was developed in phases starting around 2006. The community includes roughly 95 homes total — early townhomes followed by villa-style detached patio homes. Floor plans range from single-story to two-story, with some units offering three-car garages or elevators. Lot sizes are smaller (typically 0.07–0.16 acre), which is the trade-off for the lock-and-leave setup.

The HOA maintains landscaping and home exteriors (including painting), so the variety of brick, stone, and stucco exteriors stay consistent. Amenities include a community pool, private-access clubhouse with fitness center, walking trails, and about nine acres of common nature areas.

## Location

The neighborhood is convenient to Glade Road shopping and dining, with straightforward access to DFW Airport and the highway network. Because it’s set back and surrounded by trees, it feels quieter than the main roads nearby.

## Schools

Stone Bridge Oaks is in Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Specific campus assignments depend on the address. Confirm the current elementary, middle, and high school feed with GCISD before you buy or list.
