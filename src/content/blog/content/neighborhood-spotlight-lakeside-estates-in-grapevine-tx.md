If you've been looking at homes for sale in Grapevine, TX, Lakeside Estates is a neighborhood worth getting to know. Tucked near Grapevine Lake, this established community offers a quieter, settled feel — the kind that only comes with a couple of decades of mature trees and pride-of-ownership landscaping — without giving up easy access to everything Grapevine and the surrounding area have to offer.

## A Well-Established Community

Lakeside Estates was developed in phases between 1999 and 2002, and today it's home to roughly 269 households. Most properties sit on generous quarter-acre lots with three to four bedrooms, up to three bathrooms, and two- to three-car garages. Many homes also feature covered patios and outdoor living spaces — a nice fit for how much of the year Texas weather actually invites you outside.

## Location, Location, Location

Residents are just minutes from Grapevine Lake and Meadowmere Park, close to Historic Downtown Grapevine's shops and restaurants, and a short drive from Southlake Town Square. Commuters also appreciate the easy access to DFW-area highways, making trips to the airport or into Dallas or Fort Worth simple.

## Top-Rated Schools

Families are especially drawn to Lakeside Estates for its school assignment within the acclaimed Carroll ISD. Kids in the neighborhood attend Johnson Elementary, which feeds into Carroll Middle School and Carroll Senior High School — consistently among the top-ranked school options in the metroplex.

## A True Neighborhood Feel

Lakeside Estates has an active HOA that helps keep the community looking sharp and brings residents together throughout the year with pool parties, holiday events, and block parties. It's the kind of neighborhood where people know their neighbors — a real sense of community that's tough to put a price on.
