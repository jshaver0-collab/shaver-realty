Ashbrook Estates is a gated community in Euless, just outside Grapevine, that still falls within the Grapevine-Colleyville ISD boundaries. Most of the homes were built in the late 1990s. Buyers who are searching for homes for sale in Grapevine, TX or the surrounding Mid-Cities sometimes include it when they want GCISD schools and prefer a gated setting.

The neighborhood sits near Highway 121 and Glade Road, which gives residents reasonable access to shopping, dining, and the broader highway network. Students attend Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School. For sellers in Ashbrook Estates, the combination of the gated entrance, GCISD zoning, and convenient Mid-Cities location is usually the strongest positioning when marketing a listing.
