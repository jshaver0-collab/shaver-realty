Ashbrook Estates is a gated residential community located in Euless, just outside Grapevine, that is zoned to Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Most homes were built in the late 1990s. The neighborhood offers a more controlled access setting while still being close to Highway 121, Glade Road, and the broader Mid-Cities area.

Students attend Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School. Buyers looking at homes for sale in Grapevine, TX or the surrounding Mid-Cities often consider Ashbrook Estates when they want GCISD schools and a gated environment without being in the core of Grapevine. Sellers typically highlight the school district, the gated entrance, and the convenient highway access.
