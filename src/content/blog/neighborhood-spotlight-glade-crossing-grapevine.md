Glade Crossing is one of the more established neighborhoods that surfaces when people search for homes for sale in Grapevine, TX. Most of the single-family homes were built from the mid-1980s through the early 1990s, with a few later phases. The streets tend to be quieter, and the neighborhood has a voluntary association rather than a mandatory HOA across every section.

The location is practical. Residents are close enough to Historic Downtown Grapevine for easy access to dining and events, while still being near parks such as Hazy Meadows Park and the Pleasant Glade community pool. Students attend Grapevine Elementary, Heritage Middle School, and Colleyville Heritage High School as part of the Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Sellers in Glade Crossing often find that buyers respond well to the combination of an established neighborhood feel, GCISD schools, and a central Grapevine location.
