If you’ve been looking at homes for sale in Grapevine, TX, Western Oaks is one of the established neighborhoods that still feels like a neighborhood. Mature trees, larger floor plans, and a quiet street pattern give it a settled feel without putting you far from downtown Grapevine, everyday shopping, or DFW.

## An Established Grapevine Community

Western Oaks is not a new master-planned build. Most homes date from the mid-1980s through the late 1990s, with later custom work mixed in. The typical house is a larger single-family plan — often 3 to 5 bedrooms — on a standard suburban lot, with some properties on noticeably larger sites.

Buyers who like this pocket usually care about updates, lot position, and trees more than they care about a brand-new elevation. Sellers who have already handled roof, HVAC, kitchen, and yard work tend to stand out.

## Location, Location, Location

Western Oaks sits in Grapevine with easy access to the rest of the mid-cities. Historic Downtown Grapevine, local parks, and the highway network toward Dallas, Fort Worth, Southlake, and Colleyville are all a short drive. It is the kind of location that works if you want quieter streets without leaving the city.

## Schools

Western Oaks is in Grapevine-Colleyville ISD. Nearby campuses often discussed with this area include Heritage Elementary and the GCISD secondary feeds, but school assignment is by address. Confirm the current zoning with GCISD before you buy or list.
